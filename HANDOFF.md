# HANDOFF — SOAK_YOUR_NUTS

**State: complete and published.** Eleventh Mantra field book.

## Decisions worth not re-litigating

1. **Chapter two states the tradition's rationale as the tradition's**, not as
   verified fact. The back chapter carries the evidence. This split was a
   deliberate editorial decision and it is what lets the book be both useful
   to a raw food reader and impossible to debunk.
2. **The book never claims soaking removes anti-nutrients.** It claims better
   taste, texture, skin removal, better butter, and personal digestive
   experience — all defensible. Do not "strengthen" this later.
3. **Chapter five (food safety) stays.** Nobody else writes it and it is the
   chapter most likely to prevent actual harm.
4. **Brazil nut selenium limit stays**, without a precise per-nut figure,
   because soil variation makes one impossible.
5. **Cashews are noted as not truly raw.** Relevant and true for this audience.
6. **Warm palette, not nocturne.** Documented in the colophon and the inline
   stylesheet addendum.
7. **No `seat_plates.py` in this repo** — same reason as SELF_PUBLISHER. It
   would destroy sunlit plates.
