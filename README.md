# Soak Your Nuts

**Activating, drying, and eating nuts the slow way.**

Live: https://markoboskoauroville.github.io/SOAK_YOUR_NUTS/
Plain text: [BOOK.md](BOOK.md)

Twelve chapters, thirteen warm-daylight watercolour plates. The practice, the
timings nut by nut, the food safety, storage, and a bonus chapter on making
nut butter from activated nuts in a small personal blender.

## Different palette on purpose

The other Mantra field books are dark nocturnes. This one is **warm sunlit
watercolour** — terracotta, ochre, olive, linen, honey — because it belongs
on the permaculture / raw food / yoga shelf rather than the philosophy one.

Like `SELF_PUBLISHER`, the plates are **light images on a dark page and are
not composited**. `seat_plates.py` is deliberately absent: its border-connected
paper detector would eat a sunlit sky. Soft drop shadow instead.

## The honest structure, and why it is this way

Chapter two gives the traditional rationale — phytic acid, enzyme inhibitors,
tannins, the seed waking to water — **as the tradition holds it**, because
that is what the practice is built on and it deserves a fair statement.

*What I could not verify* then gives the published evidence in full: the ~4.75%
phytate reduction measured at UC Davis, the Lin et al. finding of higher
phytate after soaking and drying, the mineral leaching that leaves the
phytate-to-mineral ratio unchanged, and the 76-person randomised crossover
trial that found no gastrointestinal benefit.

**Do not delete either half.** The book recommends the practice on grounds
that hold — taste, texture, skins, butter, personal digestive experience —
and tells the reader plainly not to claim more than that in public. That is
what makes it usable rather than debunkable.

## Safety chapter is not optional

Chapter five covers the real risk almost no article mentions: wet nuts held at
40-46 C for 12-24 hours sit inside the bacterial growth range, which is why raw
almonds have caused salmonella outbreaks. Includes the honest raw-versus-safe
temperature trade-off. Keep it.
